QuickWord answers "what does this word mean" with the least possible
interruption: select a word in any app, tap QuickWord in the text-selection
menu, and the definition appears as a notification — no app switch, and it
works offline.

Features:

• Lookup from any app's text-selection menu or share sheet
• Notification actions: Thesaurus (swaps to synonyms in place), Open, Save
• 1.4 million words from the English Wiktionary (CC BY-SA), fully offline
• Wikipedia fallback for names and places (with attribution), with an
  optional offline pack of the 50,000 most-read articles
• Serif-typeset word pages, prefix search, recents and favourites
• Pronunciation (IPA + text-to-speech)
• No ads, no tracking, no accounts. MIT licensed.

The full dictionary (~120 MB) is downloaded once on first run; a small
starter dictionary is bundled so the app works immediately. The offline
Wikipedia pack (38 MB) is optional — get it from About → Storage & data.
